01 · What it is

A long, careful argument
for one idea.

Intelligence is becoming free. Speed is the new empathy. The brand that ships fastest wins. FRAME is the 50-page case for why — and the playbook for how.

The argument
Speed is the new empathy.

Traditional creative is priced by the hour, slowed by hand-offs. AI-native production collapses that to 48 hours. The math no longer works for agencies.

The craft
Eighteen formats every CMO should plan for.

Video is no longer a category — it contains 18 distinct formats, each with different unit economics. The atlas is in Chapter 07.

The audience
Written for the people who are still trying.

CMOs, brand directors, growth leaders, and founders — in Mumbai, Manhattan, Lagos, Singapore, São Paulo, and Bengaluru.
