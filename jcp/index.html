01 · The bet

78 stacks. Or one.

Reliance Retail runs 78 commerce channels across 10 verticals. Built channel-by-channel, that is 78 codebases, 78 release trains, 78 AI roadmaps. JCP is the bet that one stack does it instead — one codebase, one release train, one AI substrate. Every brand inherits, no brand loses its identity.

🔴

Before · the tax.

78 codebases to keep alive. 78 release trains to coordinate. Every AI capability built 78 times. Brand teams blocked on shared ops.

78× engineering tax
🟢

After · one stack.

One codebase, one release train. Vertex Search shipped to 5 channels in one cutover. Web Grounding shipped to 3 brands off one pipeline. 200M+ customers on the same substrate.

1 build → 78 channels
⚡

What it took.

Microservice tenant isolation. Strict release trains across verticals. Brand identity preserved at the edge. Infrastructure compounded at the core. AI built into the substrate.

Cataloging · Web Grounding

From 40 hours to 2 minutes per 1,000 SKUs.
