R&D · Internal Meta · Tribe v2

Tribe v2.
Predict the hit, before you ship the cut.

Meta's Tribe v2 is a foundation model of vision, audition and language for in-silico neuroscience. It predicts how the brain responds to multimodal content. The bet inside Fynd Studio: use Tribe v2 as a virality-scoring layer over generated ad creative — pick winners before spend.

Try Meta's live demo → Jump to all materials ↓ Higgsfield context ↓
01 · WHAT IT IS

A foundation model of vision, audition and language for in-silico neuroscience.

Inputs

Multimodal

Vision (image / video frames), audition (audio waveform / spectrogram), language (caption / dialogue).

Outputs

Predicted neural response

Region-by-region brain activity predictions. The "in-silico" part: simulate response without putting a person in an MRI.

Why Meta built it

Open neuroscience tool

Foundation-model scale unlocks subject-agnostic predictions — researchers can probe stimulus-response without bespoke datasets.

03 · WHY IT MATTERS · FYND STUDIO

The next moat isn't more output. It's better picking.

Fynd Studio already generates hundreds of variants per campaign. The bottleneck moved: which variant do we actually ship? A neural-response model gives us a quantitative virality prior — score every cut before media spend, route the top decile to channels.

StepTodayWith Tribe v2 layer
Generate~200 variants per briefSame — generation is solved
PickCreative director instinct + A/B in marketTribe v2 neural-response score → top 5–10 % shortlisted before spend
Spend allocationFlat per shortlisted assetWeighted by predicted response strength per cohort
Learning loopPost-campaign performancePredicted vs actual neural score → fine-tune Tribe head on our brand data

06 · INTERNAL PILOT PLAN

What to do in the next 30 days.

Week 1

Reproduce Meta's results

Clone the repo, pull weights from HF, stand up the demo on a Fynd GPU. Validate against the published benchmarks.

Week 2

Score our own creative

Run 500 historical Fynd Studio cuts (with known performance) through Tribe v2. Compare predicted score vs actual CTR / view-through.

Week 3

Side-by-side Higgsfield

Submit the same 500 cuts to Higgsfield's predictor. Compare ranking agreement, identify where the two disagree.

Week 4

Fine-tune for our brands

Train a thin head on top of Tribe v2 features using our brand × cohort performance data. Target: outperform off-the-shelf on Fynd brands by > 15 % rank correlation.

Day 30 · gate

Ship or shelf?

If the fine-tuned head beats CD picks on at least 3 of 5 brand benchmarks, ship as a Fynd Studio gate. Otherwise document and revisit next quarter.

Open questions

What we still need to know

Licence terms for commercial use of the weights · inference cost per asset at our scale · whether Meta plans a hosted API.
