01 · Storefront

Building Blocks. API-First. No-Code.

Pre-built modules that mix and match into shopping journeys tailored to your brand. Every core capability exposed as an API for developers; a no-code configuration layer for business users.

01.01

Building Blocks

Pre-built modules merchants can compose into journeys tailored to brand and business logic.

01.02

API-First Tool

Every core commerce capability as an API. Build custom storefronts, apps, and tools without integration drift on upgrades.

01.03

No-Code Tool

Business users change layouts, branding, experiences via simple configuration screens. Faster experiments, less tech-team dependency.

01.04

Extensions & Themes

Reorganized screens grouping items by type and location. Merchants fine-tune storefront look and behavior in fewer clicks.

01.05

Collection Management

Product collections that auto-update based on rules or editorial picks. Storefront stays fresh without manual tagging.

01.06

UI Error Handling

Catches misconfigurations early inside the UI. Reduces broken flows and edge-case failures.

01.07

Launch Essentials

Newsletter capture, catalog sync, analytics hooks — the basics needed to get a trackable store live quickly.

01.08

First Sale Toolkit

Visibility, operations, and reporting extensions to secure the first orders and understand performance gaps.

01.09

Customer Support

WhatsApp notifications, communication helpers — customers stay informed about orders and support.

01.10

Public Newsroom

Brand-side site for press releases and updates. Centralizes communication for media, partners, stakeholders.

01.11

Launch Site Builder

Templates for quickly creating sites for new launches. Time-to-live drops from weeks to days.

01.12

360° Customer Profile

Purchases, preferences, interactions in one place. Staff personalize service and offers in context.

01.13

Integrated Wishlist

Customers save products to revisit. Powers retargeting, reminders, repeat visits.

01.14

Purchase Tracking

Clear record of past orders and behavior. Powers analytics, service resolution, tailored campaigns.

02 · Payments

Smart routing, every method, five markets.

Respect the customer's preferred method, fall back gracefully, route to the best gateway, and reconcile across Jio Cinema and JioMart with one wallet. Live for India, UAE, US, Canada, UK out of the box.

02.01

Payment Management

Respect customer's preferred method, gracefully switch to backups on failure. Higher checkout success, lower drop-offs.

02.02

Cross-Platform Wallet

Shared wallet and consolidated billing across Jio Cinema and JioMart for simpler reconciliation.

02.03

Enterprise Workflows

Business credit lines, limits, approvals in real time. APIs link JCP with SAP/ERPs for POs, vendor onboarding, O2C flows.

02.04

Indian Tax Dashboard

Track and report tax compliance in one place. Simplifies audits and regulatory filings.

02.05

Invoice Generation

Automatic GST calculation and compliant live invoices. Errors drop, speed rises.

02.06

Map Settings

Visually define delivery areas on a map. Manage where you deliver and how you price it.

02.07

Flexible Fulfillment

Orders auto-routed to the best store or warehouse by rules. Optimizes cost, speed, stock usage.

02.08

Real-Time Delivery

Live tracking of shipments and delivery status. Transparent for customers and support teams.

02.09

Payment Routing

Traffic dynamically sent to the best-performing gateway. Optimizes for success-rate and cost.

02.10

Smart Retry

Separates temporary network blips from permanent failures. Retries only when they can succeed.

02.11

Transaction Status

Clear real-time views of payment outcomes in ops tools and customer pages. Less support overhead.

02.12

Refunds

Better flows for COD and split-payment refunds with clearer tracking. Faster resolution, more trust.

02.13

India · UAE · US · Canada · UK

Built-in currency, payment methods, tax rules for five geographies. Go live without building local rails.

03 · Intelligence

Real-time storytelling. AI inside every workflow.

Dashboards that auto-convert raw data into clear written insights for non-analysts. AI customer support, AI alignment, and AI-on-green-energy data centres — all powered by shared group-level intelligence.

03.01

Real-Time Storytelling

Live-updating dashboards that automatically convert raw data into clear written insights. Non-analysts understand performance.

03.02

KPI Tracking

Dashboards measuring checkout flow performance. Identifies bottlenecks, improves conversion.

03.03

JioFrames & JioStar

AI tools blending commerce and media — smart recommendations in content. Higher engagement and conversion via context.

03.04

Open Telecom Platform

Partner-backed AI infrastructure: AMD, Cisco, Nokia. High-speed inference and robust ops at scale.

03.05

AI Customer Support

Virtual agents and tools that assist human agents with summaries, intent detection, next-best actions.

03.06

AI Alignment

Integrated with the central AI platform. Features powered by shared group-level intelligence, not bespoke models.

03.07

AI + Green-Energy DCs

Workloads served from green-energy data centres. Performance with footprint accounted for.

04 · Security

Production-grade safety, end-to-end.

Data protection, threat prevention, disaster recovery, and governance — all standardized to enterprise expectations, audited continuously.

04.01

Data Protection

Encryption at rest and in transit. Strict role-based access controls. Secure authentication mechanisms. Sensitive information isolated end-to-end.

04.02

Threat Prevention

Continuous security monitoring, intrusion detection, vulnerability assessments. Risks identified and mitigated in real time.

04.03

Disaster Recovery

Cloud-native architecture with load balancing, redundancy, automated backups. Designed to minimize downtime and protect data integrity.

04.04

Governance Framework

Aligned to industry-standard security and data governance. SDLC processes, periodic compliance reviews, controls strengthened continuously.

05 · Build

Visual canvas. Open SDKs. Self-serve partners.

A drag-and-drop canvas for non-developers, GraphQL for system connections, local-runner deployment for security and control. Boltic, GLAM AR, and a partner marketplace round out the build stack.

05.01

Tracking & Timeline

Central view of what's launching when, with dependencies and maintenance windows for risk and rollback. Teams stay aligned.

05.02

Hub & Version Control

Automatic updates to relevant teams and tight linkage to code versions. Cleaner cutovers.

05.03

Visual Canvas Interface

Drag-and-drop workflow design for non-developers. GraphQL for system connections. Local runner deployment for control.

05.04

Summarised Run History

Detailed logs of each workflow execution. Powers debugging, auditing, optimization.

05.05

Boltic

05.06

GLAM AR

Easy-to-use SDKs to add AR features across Web, React Native, Flutter, Android, iOS.

05.07

Self-Serve Integration

Marketplace where partners list apps and merchants install them. Grows integrations without custom work.

05.08

Partner Onboarding

Streamlined onboarding flows with verification. Faster partner go-lives, less manual ops.

05.09

Revenue Sharing

Built-in logic for calculating and distributing partner commissions. Transparent and scalable.

05.10

Cloud Deployment

Each service packaged and deployed in a standard way, auto-scales with demand. Better performance, less manual infra work.

05.11

Catalog Performance

Optimized queries, pagination, caching for stores with very large catalogs. Browsing stays fast at million-SKU scale.

Try-on. Skin analysis. AI media. Reels in the page.

Virtual try-on, AI skin analysis, image and video generation from text — plus floating shoppable Reels embedded right in the page. Engagement designed for the categories where the world sells the product.

06.01

06.02

AI Skin Analysis

06.03

06.04

Motion & Video Generation

06.05

Press & Studio

AI tools to generate and edit video content. Faster campaign and content creation.

06.06

SaleAssist · Reels

Floating Reels-style video widgets embedded directly on the site. Boost engagement and conversion with rich, interactive content.
