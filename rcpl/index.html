03 · Architecture · six platform surfaces

One DMS,
six entry points.

The same data backbone serves every stakeholder — from RCPL business ops to the field CSO to the BPO team — through a purpose-fit interface for each role.

  • Admin Portal
    Bulk onboarding, pricing & scheme rules, article-market mapping, data-correction scripts.
  • Distributor Platform
    5-step primary ordering with FTL guidance, credit check, invoice & picklist downloads.
  • CSO App · Samruddhi
    Beat plan, secondary orders, OTP-verified retailer onboarding, GSTIN auto-populate.
  • Saarthi · Assisted Ordering
    ASM/ASE places primary orders on behalf of distributors via mobile-web with auto-login.
  • CTS · Cooler Tracking
    12-stage asset lifecycle across 10 stakeholder roles — QR-scan install to BPO closure.
