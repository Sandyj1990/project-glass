The problem it solves

NSO ran on email + spreadsheets.
ALP replaces all of it.

Document exchange over email, scattered site-assessment notes with no audit trail, no single state-by-state AOP funnel. ALP gives every candidate site one record — lead creation through legal, fit-out, and payments — with role-based access scoped on login.
