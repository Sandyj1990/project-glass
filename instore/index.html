07 · WHERE TEAMS GET STUCK

The four objections you'll hear in the first week.

Every team that has adopted these platforms has run into the same four worries. Here is how it actually plays out.

Pitfall #1

"Our store managers don't want another dashboard."

Most store managers already have 6 dashboards they don't open. Adding a 7th is correctly resisted. Retail Jarvis sidesteps this — the scores go to the regional ops lead, not the store manager. Action goes to the store manager via the channel they already use (WhatsApp, the floor walk).

The move: Don't roll out a new UI to the store. Roll out actions to the store. The dashboard is for HQ; the store manager just gets clearer asks.
Pitfall #2

"Site selection is an art. Retail Vista will pick wrong."

Possibly true once. The A/B in the 30/60/90 plan exists for exactly this. After 12 months of 25-vs-25 comparison, the team has data on where intuition out-performs scoring (rare local micro-context) and where scoring out-performs intuition (sector-level demand patterns).

The move: Don't replace the team's judgement — augment it. Use Retail Vista to surface candidates the team would have missed and to disqualify candidates the team was falsely-positive about. The final pick stays human.
Pitfall #3

"Robots in the aisle will disturb customers."

AutRi robots run during off-hours (early morning, late evening, overnight) for exactly this reason. Customer-facing hours stay clean. The compliance check happens when the floor is empty.

The move: Schedule the robot windows around store traffic. Customer-facing operations notice nothing. Compliance happens; customer experience is undisturbed.
Pitfall #4

"Frontline staff won't engage with another HR app."

Samarth is integrated into People First — the platform frontline staff already use for shifts, pay slips, leave requests. They don't install another app; they see a new tab. The career-path visualisation is the hook — for the first time, the path is concrete and personal.

The move: Don't market Samarth as a new platform. Position it as a new feature of People First. Adoption follows.
08 · FAQ

The questions you'll get from your team.

Can Retail Vista work for stores we already operate, not just new ones?

Yes. The geospatial scoring also identifies under-performing existing stores by site quality vs realized performance. Useful for rationalisation decisions and for understanding what to fix at an existing location.

What does AutRi cost per store?

Per-store cost depends on store size, shelf-count, and run frequency. The Powai pilot is the cleanest reference — talk to the Autri team for current per-store economics.

Does Samarth Plus replace our existing LMS?

Samarth runs inside People First as an extension, not a replacement. SCORM-compliant content libraries port over. The career-pathway layer is the new piece.

Can Forge run for D2C brands Reliance acquires?

Yes — Forge is brand-agnostic. The current production deployment is at NeoLync Tirupati for Reliance's own-brand factories. The pattern extends to any acquired brand with its own manufacturing footprint.

What about Neo Tailor inside an existing store?

The micro-factory model is designed for a store-attached or city-attached deployment. The Mumbai cluster MTM piece runs as a 7-day cycle. Roadmap includes a store-embedded variant for flagship locations.

How does Retail Jarvis collect signal without intruding on customers?

Predictive non-intrusive sensing — uses existing in-store telemetry (POS, footfall counters, CCTV-derived movement patterns where deployed), not customer surveys. The four scores are derived without a single customer prompt.
